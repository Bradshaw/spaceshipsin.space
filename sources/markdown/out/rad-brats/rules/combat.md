# Combat

## Initiative d6
- 1-3 Enemies begin
- 4-6 Player characters begin

## Fighting

### Attacking
Pick a target, roll to hit, and if successful, roll weapon damage
**Melee:** CH12 Muscles
*Power through: nearby enemies may attack the PC*
**Ranged:** CH12 Smarts

Improvised weapons deal d4 damage
Bare fists deal d2

#### Crit (natural 20)
Double damage, reduce armour/protection by one tier

#### Fumble (natural 1)
**Melee:** Enemy gains a free attack
**Ranged:** Weapon breaks, it can be repaired in a moment of calm

### Defending
Roll CH12 Moves, if failure, the enemy rolls weapon damage
Shields reduce damage by 1, or can block all damage and break
Armour reduces damage depending on tier:
- Tier 1: -d2
- Tier 2: -d4
- Tier 3: -d6

#### Crit (natural 20)
Gain a free attack

#### Fumble (natural 1)
Douple damage, armour is reduced by one tier


## Whooped (0HP)
Gain a whooped point, and roll as many d6 as you have whooped points, take the lowest:
- 6-4: Shook: Come back next turn with d4 HP
- 2-3 Bashed: Out for d4 rounds, come back with d4 HP
- 1: Schooled: Roll on the sick scars table, out for d4 rounds, come back with d4 HP
- Multiple 1s: Absolutely Dunked On: Totally humiliated, you're no longer cool enough to be an adventurer, go home...
