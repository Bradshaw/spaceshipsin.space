# Lifeform differentiation
Mutants are biological animals
Dirtlings are biological plants and fungi
Robots are mechanical
Cyborgs are a mix of biological animal and mechanical

## Survival
Biological creatures need water and food
Mechanical creatures need batteries
Cyborgs need water and either food or batteries?

## Healing
Biological creatures take time to heal, but it happens naturally
Mechanical creatures can be repaired, which takes resources
I guess cyborgs can pick and choose?
