# Magickal Mishaps

1. MB: You and a random nearby creature pass out.
    *When you wake up your souls have switched.*
2. MB: d6 arms sprout from your back, they are mischevous and cruel.
3. MB: The magick works, but it is twisted to your disadvantage.
4. MB-ish: The scroll explodes in your hands, you and nearby creatures make a CH14 Guts test or lose d6 HP.
5. MB: The magick works, but it draws energy from your lifeforce.
    *When resting you only restore half of the rolled HP.*
6. You sink into the ground and get stuck waist-deep in solid rock.
7. Weapons burn your hands.
    *Take d2 damage each round for each weapon held in hand and not immediately dropped.*
8. Your mind and spirit swap places.
    *Damage and healing affect your magicks. Casting magicks draws from your HP. Falling to 0 magicks whoops you.*
9.  Magickal energy flows through you uncontrollably.
    *Restore d2 magicks every few minutes. Take d4 damage whenever magick overflows.*
10. (??) Fear and love become intertwined.
    *You become unable to tell friend from foe.*
11. An overconfident spirit takes up residence in your mind.
    *You randomly blurt out taunts and threats, looking for a fight.*
12. You crackle with static electricity.
    *Whenever a creature touches you, you both make a CH14 Guts test of lose d2 HP.*
13. You start to glow. Nice! You can see things when it's dark!
    *And creatures can see you too...*
14. Your body begins to slowly and subtly fluctuate in size.
    *Your clothes will never fit you again.*
15. You miscast the magick, cast a random magick from the other table.
16. You disappear from existence.
    *You reappear in the same spot in d20 minutes.*
17. Your bag feels slightly heavier.
    *A cursed gem has appeared in your bag. If you discard it, it returns, slightly darker, within d6 days.*
18. You feel gravity pulling at you erratically.
    *Whenever you roll a natural 1, you are flung d6 metres in a random direction.*
19. Your shadow detaches from you and attacks.
    *Shadow: 4 HP, Defence CH16, Smother (d6).*
20. A little pink clound begins to circle above your head.
    *It repeats everything you say in a mocking tone.*
