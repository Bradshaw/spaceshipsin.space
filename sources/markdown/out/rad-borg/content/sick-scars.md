# Sick Scars

A Sick Scar can be healed to remove its **hindrance** and gain its *bonus*, the *bonus* isn't available until the scar is healed.
Sick Scars are for life, even if they are healed, whether they're visible or not, and whether or not they're providing **hindrances** or *bonuses*.
If you roll the same Sick Scar a second time, it returns to its unhealed state.

1. Face gash **Looks nasty** *Looks cool*
2. Sprained leg **-1 Moves** *Hyper flexible legs*
3. Concussion **-1 Smarts** *Learn a weird new party trick*
4. Winded **-1 Muscles** *You're real good at push-ups*
5. Beaten up **-1 Guts** *You have a cool limp*
6. Shaken **Roll a d6 when Flexing. On a 1: lose the Flex and the GM may use it against you instead** *Reroll one of your Cool Vibes*
7.  Broken bones **-1 Moves** *+1 Smarts*
8.  Head wound! Head wound! **-1 Smarts** *+1 Guts*
9.  Gnarly chest gash **-1 Muscles** *+ Moves*
10. Oh dang, you got battered! **-1 Guts** *+1 Muscles*
11. Look mum, no hand! **One-handed** *Single-handedly wield a two-handed weapon with your powered prothetic arm*
12. Haunted **You feel strangely detached from this world** *Learn a random fresh spell that you can cast without a scroll*
