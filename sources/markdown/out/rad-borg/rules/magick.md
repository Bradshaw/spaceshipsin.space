# Magick

To cast magick:
- Hold a scroll in hand
- Spend a magick point
- Make a Smarts CH12 test
- Failure means the magick doesn't work, the caster becomes dizzy for one hour
    - [Power through](./power-through): The PC can choose to roll on the [Magickal Mishaps](../content/magickal-mishaps) table and cast the magick anyway
- Fumble or failing when dizzy: Roll on the [Magickal Mishaps](../content/magickal-mishaps) table
